# Ticket Gate

Use this gate after ticket creation or refinement and before implementation planning.

## Purpose

Make sure the previous step is actually complete and that the user explicitly confirmed it is good enough to continue.

## Checklist

- The ticket text has been drafted or refined in full.
- Scope, and acceptance criteria are concrete enough for planning.
- Missing information and assumptions have been surfaced clearly.
- The final ticket text was shown to the user.
- The user explicitly confirmed the ticket is ready to proceed.
- Jira was created or updated if this stage required it.

## Rule

Do not continue to implementation planning until the user explicitly confirms that the ticket stage is complete or explicitly says to proceed.
